# Changelog

All notable changes to this mod are documented here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the mod uses
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-29

### Added

- A third STATS page with each DV (HP derived the way Gen 1 does it) and each
  Stat Exp value, with a ★ on any perfect 15.
- A DV total out of 60 and a GREAT / GOOD / OK / POOR grade.
- The DV page opens by itself right after a catch, whether the Pokémon joins
  the party or goes to a PC box.
